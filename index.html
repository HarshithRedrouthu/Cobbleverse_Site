About the Devs

Welcome to LUMYVERSE! What started as a way to bridge a 760-kilometer gap in a long-distance relationship quickly blossomed into a vibrant, global home for gamers. Created by EL (Luca) and Mary, LUMYVERSE is built on one fundamental idea: being together. EL, a lifelong gamer with a passion for creative projects and making people smile, and Mary, a fiercely competitive co-op enthusiast who tackles life with the energy of a volcano ready to erupt, joined forces to build this unique virtual space. Here, players are never just numbers—they are friends sharing in the fun, learning new things, and celebrating unforgettable victories side by side.
